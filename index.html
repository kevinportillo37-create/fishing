          Best days ahead

            All times are Central Time.

            Major windows run one hour either side of the moon passing overhead or underfoot. Minor windows run 30 minutes either side of moonrise and moonset. Ratings weigh the moon phase, windows that land on sunrise or sunset, and how close the moon is to Earth.

            The game plan uses common Texas inshore patterns for fishing from land. They're rules of thumb, not guarantees. Check TPWD rules before keeping fish.

            Tide predictions, water level, wind, and water temperature come from NOAA Tides & Currents. The forecast comes from the National Weather Service. Moon and sun times are calculated on your phone, so they work offline.
